New players enchant randomly and waste 30 levels of XP on pointless combinations. Then they run out of levels when they actually need good enchantments.

Experienced players know exactly which enchantments matter and in what order. They prioritize the ones that save time. Everything else is optional.

The difference isn't knowledge—it's priority. Know what to enchant first, and you'll never waste XP again.

## The Enchantment Tier System

Not all enchantments are equally valuable. Some save you hours. Others are nice-to-haves.

**Tier 1 (MUST HAVE):**
- Mending
- Unbreaking III
- Protection IV (armor only)

**Tier 2 (SHOULD HAVE):**
- Efficiency V (pickaxe)
- Sharpness V (sword)
- Feather Falling IV (boots)
- Thorns III (armor, debatable)

**Tier 3 (NICE-TO-HAVE):**
- Fortune III (pickaxe, optional if you have Mending)
- Knockback II (sword, situational)
- Fire Aspect (sword, purely aesthetic)
- Aqua Affinity (helmet, for underwater mining)

**Tier 4 (SKIP):**
- Frost Walker (boots)
- Curse of Vanishing
- Curse of Binding

## Why Mending is Non-Negotiable

Mending is the single most important enchantment. Here's why:

**Without Mending:** Your diamond pickaxe lasts ~1,500 blocks before breaking. You craft a new one. You've spent 5 diamonds, 10 sticks, and inventory space.

**With Mending:** Your pickaxe lasts forever (it repairs using XP drops). You never lose it.

The time saved: 5 diamonds per broken pickaxe × 50 pickaxes per year = 250 diamonds saved annually. That's *years* of mining eliminated.

How to get it: Kill mobs that drop XP while holding the item you want to repair. Mending triggers automatically—it absorbs XP that would go to your level bar and repairs the tool instead.

**Priority:** Mending on pickaxe first. Then sword. Then chestplate. This order matters because you use pickaxes and swords most.

## Unbreaking III Extends Tool Life

Unbreaking doesn't prevent breaking—it reduces durability loss.

**Without Unbreaking:** Pickaxe lasts 1,500 blocks (1,562 hits).

**With Unbreaking III:** Pickaxe lasts ~6,000 blocks. That's 4x longer.

Why this matters: Between Mending repairs, Unbreaking III reduces the frequency of drops you need. You get less XP available for other priorities. Unbreaking reduces that pressure.

**How to use:** Put Unbreaking III on your main tools—pickaxe, sword, shovel. Skip it on throwaway items (tools you don't care about).

## Protection IV on Armor (Pick ONE Type)

Protection IV reduces all damage types by 4% per piece. With 4 pieces (full set), that's 16% reduction.

**Full netherite with Protection IV:** Takes 8 hearts of damage from a creeper. Without Protection IV: takes 9.5 hearts. The difference feels small until you're in a fight.

**Where it matters:** Nether exploration, End fights, cave diving. Anywhere hostile mobs concentrate.

**Where it doesn't:** Farming, building, peaceful exploration. Damage is rare; Protection is wasted.

**Priority:** Get it on chestplate first (highest coverage). Then legs. Then helmet. Skip boots (Feather Falling is better).

## Efficiency V Pickaxe (For Mining Speed)

Efficiency V mines blocks 2x faster than an unenchanted pickaxe.

**Without Efficiency:** Mining a stone block takes 0.75 seconds.

**With Efficiency V:** Same block takes 0.15 seconds.

Over an hour of mining, that's 30+ minutes saved. Massive difference.

**Trade-off:** Efficiency uses XP that could go to Mending repairs. If your pickaxe has both Mending + Efficiency V, you'll need lots of XP sources (mob grinder, spawner farm, etc).

**Practical solution:** Get Mending on your main pickaxe. Efficiency V is a bonus if you have XP to spare. Don't skip Mending for it.

## Sharpness V Sword (For Combat Damage)

Sharpness V adds 2.5 damage per hit to your sword.

**Without Sharpness:** Sword does 7 damage per hit (diamond sword baseline).

**With Sharpness V:** Sword does 9.5 damage per hit.

This kills mobs ~30% faster. In fights with Wardens or groups of mobs, that's the difference between surviving and dying.

**Where it matters:** End fights (Ender Dragon), Warden encounters, raiding mob spawners. Combat-heavy activities.

**Where it doesn't:** Mining, farming, peaceful exploration.

**Priority:** Lower than Mending/Unbreaking. Combat situations are rare compared to mining situations. Get it once you have main gear sorted.

## Feather Falling IV Boots (Mandatory)

Feather Falling reduces fall damage by 12% per level. Level IV = 48% reduction.

**Without Feather Falling:** Falling 6 blocks kills you.

**With Feather Falling IV:** Falling 6 blocks deals 2 hearts damage (survivable).

Seems small until you're exploring caves and accidentally fall into a ravine. Feather Falling doesn't save your life in *most* situations. But in the critical situations where you slip and fall, it does.

**Why prioritize it:** Cheap to get (only Level IV). Mending handles tool durability. Protection handles mob damage. Feather Falling handles the unexpected falls that kill casual players constantly.

## Aqua Affinity Helmet (For Underwater Mining)

Aqua Affinity removes the underwater mining slowdown.

**Without Aqua Affinity:** Mining underwater is 5x slower.

**With Aqua Affinity:** Mining is normal speed.

**When you need it:** Only when mining underwater (rare). Most mining happens on land. Even most underwater loot can be grabbed without mining.

**Priority:** Very low. Only get this if you plan to build underwater or farm underwater resources. Otherwise, skip it.

## What NOT to Enchant (Time Wasters)

**Curse of Vanishing:** Makes your item disappear when you die. Pointless if you use Mending (you won't die). Pointless if you don't use Mending (you'll lose the item anyway).

**Curse of Binding:** Item can't be removed once equipped. Traps you in armor you wanted to replace. Only useful for trolling.

**Frost Walker:** Makes ice form under your feet when walking on water. Looks cool. Breaks redirects and ice farms. Skip it.

**Fire Aspect (Sword):** Makes mobs burn. Deals 1-2 extra damage. Mobs die fast anyway. The fire is visual fluff.

**Knockback II (Sword):** Knocks mobs back when hit. Sounds useful. In practice, mobs come back and you waste time hitting them multiple times. Not worth the XP.

## The Optimal Enchantment Path

**First 50 XP levels:**
1. Mending on pickaxe
2. Unbreaking III on pickaxe
3. Efficiency V on pickaxe

Result: Indestructible, fast mining pickaxe. Your primary tool.

**Next 30 XP levels:**
4. Mending on sword
5. Sharpness V on sword

Result: Indestructible damage weapon for combat.

**Next 20 XP levels:**
6. Protection IV on chestplate
7. Feather Falling IV on boots

Result: Armor that survives hits and falls.

**Total: 100 XP levels for core gear.** Everything else is luxury.

## Getting XP Fast

You need lots of XP to enchant properly. Where do you get it?

**Best sources:**
- Mob grinder (spawner farm): 500+ XP per hour
- Cave diving (killing mobs): 50-100 XP per hour
- Smelting (no killing, just XP drops): 10-20 XP per hour
- Fishing: 1-2 XP per fish (tedious)

Mob grinders are the clear winner. One grinder provides enough XP for thousands of enchantments.

## FAQ

**Q: Should I use Silk Touch or Fortune III on my pickaxe?**
A: With Mending, neither matters much. Silk Touch lets you pick up ore blocks and smelt later (useful for storage). Fortune multiplies drops (useful for XP efficiency). Pick Silk Touch for storage, Fortune for direct drops. Or use an enchanting table to get both if RNG allows.

**Q: Can I have Mending and Unbreaking on the same tool?**
A: Yes. In fact, you should. Unbreaking extends time between Mending repairs. Both on one tool = indestructible + rare maintenance.

**Q: Is Thorns worth enchanting on armor?**
A: Thorns deals 4% of damage back to attackers (with Thorns III). Sounds good. In practice, mobs die so fast from your sword that Thorns barely triggers. Skip it unless you like the idea more than the efficiency.

**Q: What if I only have 10 XP levels?**
A: Get Mending on your pickaxe. That single enchantment saves more time than anything else. Everything else can wait.

**Q: Should I enchant diamond or netherite gear?**
A: Netherite. It's identical after enchanting, just more durable. You'll eventually upgrade to netherite anyway, so enchant that from the start.

## Final Thoughts

Most players waste XP on enchantments that sound good but don't matter. Mending, Unbreaking, and Protection are the trinity. Everything else is negotiable.

Prioritize tools over armor (you use them more). Prioritize Mending over everything (it's never not useful). Skip the cosmetic enchantments entirely.

Enchant smart and your gear lasts forever. Enchant randomly and you're farming XP constantly.
