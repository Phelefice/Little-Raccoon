Woodland Mansions are one of the most dangerous structures in Minecraft. They spawn rarely in dark forest biomes, they are enormous, and they are filled with Vindicators and Evokers that hit hard and fast. Most players who walk in unprepared walk back out with nothing — or do not walk out at all.

This guide covers everything: how to find a Woodland Mansion, how to prepare for it, how to clear it room by room, and what loot to expect.

## What Is a Woodland Mansion

A Woodland Mansion is a massive dark oak structure that spawns exclusively in dark forest biomes. It is three stories tall, contains dozens of rooms, and is guarded by Illagers — specifically Vindicators and Evokers, two of the most dangerous mobs in the game.

Inside, you will find chests with valuable loot, a chance at the Totem of Undying from Evokers, and in some rooms, hidden secrets including diamond blocks concealed behind walls.

## How to Find a Woodland Mansion

### Using a Cartographer Villager

The most reliable method is trading with a Cartographer villager until they offer a **Woodland Mansion Map**. This map shows the direction and approximate location of the nearest mansion. Cartographers reach this trade at journeyman level (green badge).

The map costs around 14 emeralds plus a compass. It is worth every emerald — mansions can be thousands of blocks from spawn, and wandering dark forests randomly is extremely inefficient.

### Using a Command

In a world where you have cheats enabled, use:
`/locate structure minecraft:mansion`

This returns the coordinates of the nearest Woodland Mansion instantly.

### Exploring Dark Forests

If you prefer exploration, look for dark forest biomes — the biome with large dark oak trees and a dark canopy. Mansions always spawn in these biomes. They are large enough to be visible above the treeline once you are close enough.

## How to Prepare

Do not go to a Woodland Mansion without the following:

**Armor and weapons:** Full iron armor at minimum, diamond preferred. Enchant your armor with Protection IV if possible. Bring a diamond or netherite sword with Sharpness.

**Shield:** Mandatory. Vindicators deal 7-19 damage per hit depending on difficulty. A shield blocks all of that. Never enter without one.

**Food:** Bring at least 64 cooked meat or golden carrots. You will take damage constantly.

**Bow and arrows:** Essential for dealing with Evokers from a distance before they summon Vexes.

**Torches:** Lots of them. Lighting up the mansion as you clear it prevents mobs from respawning in cleared areas.

**Beds:** Optional but useful if you want to set your spawn inside or nearby before pushing deeper.

**Potions:** Strength and Regeneration potions make the fight significantly easier. Fire Resistance is useful in some room traps.

## Clearing the Mansion Room by Room

### General Strategy

The mansion is three floors of interconnected rooms. The key rule is: **clear from the outside in**. Circle the exterior first, then work inward floor by floor. Never push into a dark room without lighting it first.

Move slowly. Open one door at a time. If you hear Vindicators nearby, prepare your shield before opening the door.

### Vindicators

Vindicators are axe-wielding Illagers that deal massive melee damage. They are fast and aggressive.

Strategy: Keep your shield raised when they approach. Block their first hit, then strike back. Two or three hits with a Sharpness sword is enough. Never let multiple Vindicators surround you — back into a doorway so only one can reach you at a time.

### Evokers

Evokers are spellcasters that summon Vexes and create fang attacks on the ground. They are the most dangerous mob in the mansion.

Strategy: As soon as you see an Evoker, shoot it with your bow before it can cast. If it summons Vexes, deal with the Vexes first — they are small and annoying but die quickly. Then finish the Evoker. Every Evoker drops a **Totem of Undying**, which prevents death once when held in your off-hand. Always collect these.

### Vexes

Vexes are small flying mobs summoned by Evokers. They can fly through walls and deal surprising damage. Kill them quickly with a sword — they have low health but are annoying in groups.

## Hidden Secrets

### Diamond Blocks Behind Walls

Some rooms in the mansion have hidden diamond blocks concealed behind wool or stone walls. Look for rooms where the wall material seems inconsistent — a section of wool or cobblestone that does not match the surrounding oak walls often hides something behind it.

Common locations include the jail room (wool walls hide diamonds behind them) and a secret room accessible by breaking through certain walls on the upper floors.

### Chest Loot

Mansion chests contain some of the best early-to-mid game loot available:

- Diamond armor pieces and tools
- Enchanted books
- Music discs
- Golden apples
- Redstone and lapis in bulk
- Name tags and leads
- Buckets and iron in quantity

Not every room has a chest — most rooms are just encounter spaces. The chests that do exist are usually in dedicated storage rooms or hidden chambers.

## After Clearing the Mansion

Once cleared, a Woodland Mansion makes an excellent base. It is enormous, already structured into rooms, and in a dramatic biome. Convert it into your main base or a storage facility.

The mansion also respawns Illagers over time if not lit up properly. After clearing, place torches in every room and hallway to prevent respawning. Check dark corners and unlit corridors — missed torches invite new spawns.

## FAQ

**Q: Can Woodland Mansions spawn near each other?**
A: Very rarely. Mansions have a minimum separation distance built into world generation. In most worlds you will only encounter one within a reasonable exploration range.

**Q: Do mobs respawn inside the mansion after I clear it?**
A: Yes, if the mansion is not fully lit. Any area with light level 7 or below will spawn hostile mobs. Place torches everywhere after clearing to prevent this.

**Q: Is the Totem of Undying guaranteed from Evokers?**
A: Yes. Every Evoker drops exactly one Totem of Undying when killed. Clearing a full mansion typically yields 3-5 totems depending on how many Evokers spawned.

**Q: Can I raid a Woodland Mansion in early game?**
A: Technically yes, but it is very risky. Full iron armor and a good sword are the absolute minimum. Diamond armor and enchantments make the experience dramatically safer.

## Conclusion

A Woodland Mansion raid is one of the most rewarding mid-game challenges in Minecraft. The Totems of Undying alone make it worth the effort — having multiple totems in your inventory is a safety net that changes how you approach dangerous situations throughout the rest of the game.

Prepare thoroughly, move carefully, and light every room as you go. The mansion rewards patience over aggression.
