## What to Look for in a Trial Chamber Seed

The best seeds combine several factors: a Trial Chamber within 500 blocks of spawn (close enough to reach quickly but not so close it feels handed to you), a decent spawn biome with early resources, and ideally other structures nearby that support your progression toward the Trial Chamber.

Trial Chambers generate in the Deepslate layer between Y=-20 and Y=-40. The seeds below include coordinates so you know exactly where to dig.

## Tips for Playing Trial Chamber Seeds

### Prepare Before Entering

Even with a Trial Chamber close to spawn, do not rush in unprepared. Spend the first day gathering iron for full armor and a shield. Breezes deal significant damage and Bogged poison arrows are relentless. Going in with leather armor is a death sentence.

### Bring Extra Keys

You earn one Trial Key per player per Trial Spawner. Solo players get one key per spawner. Bring a friend and each player gets their own key from the same spawner — doubling your Vault openings without requiring any extra chambers.

### Ominous Trials Strategy

To activate Ominous Trials, kill a Pillager Raid Captain in the overworld first (they carry a banner on their back). Entering a Trial Chamber with Bad Omen converts all Trial Spawners to Ominous versions. The mobs are harder but the loot is significantly better, including Ominous Trial Keys for Ominous Vaults where the Heavy Core drops.

### Save Ominous Keys for the Right Vaults

Ominous Vaults look different from regular Vaults — they have a darker, more ornate appearance. Use your Ominous Trial Keys only on Ominous Vaults. Regular Vaults accept only regular Trial Keys.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these seeds work on Bedrock Edition?**
A: These seeds are tested on Java Edition 1.21. Bedrock Edition uses different world generation, so the same seed produces a different world. Bedrock players should search for Bedrock-specific Trial Chamber seeds.

**Q: Can I use these seeds on an existing world?**
A: No — seeds only apply at world creation. You cannot change the seed of an existing world.

**Q: What happens if I cannot find the Trial Chamber at the listed coordinates?**
A: Confirm you are on Java Edition 1.21 and entered the seed exactly. Even a single character difference produces a completely different world. Coordinates can also vary slightly between minor version updates.

**Q: How deep do I need to mine to reach a Trial Chamber?**
A: Trial Chambers generate between Y=-20 and Y=-40. From the surface at sea level (Y=64), that means mining down 84-104 blocks. Following existing cave systems is faster than strip mining straight down.
